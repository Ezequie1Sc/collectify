# Collectify

> **AI-first business intelligence and inventory platform for real-world retail.**

Collectify is an inventory and business management platform designed for a real local store that sells anime and pop-culture collectibles such as Funko, Pokémon products, plushies, pins, posters, and other merchandise.

The project was created for the **Hacktoberfest 2026 DEV Challenge**, while being developed around a real business need: helping a local store organize inventory, sales, partners, and purchasing decisions in one place.

## The problem

A store with multiple partners needs more than a simple stock counter. It needs to know:

- Which products belong to each partner.
- How much inventory each partner has.
- What products are selling.
- How much money is being generated.
- Which products may need restocking.
- Where to find potential suppliers.
- How to make decisions using the information generated by the business.

Collectify is designed to centralize this information and turn it into actionable business intelligence.

## The idea behind Collectify

**We did not build an inventory system and then add an AI chatbot.**

**We built a business platform where AI is a core component that transforms business information into useful solutions.**

AI is integrated into actual workflows such as:

- Business analysis.
- Product and sales analysis.
- Supplier discovery.
- Purchasing research.
- Inventory decisions.
- Recommendations based on business data.

## Main features

### Inventory management

Products can be organized using information such as:

- Product name and description.
- Category.
- SKU.
- Cost and sale price.
- Stock.
- Product status.
- Product ownership.

### Multi-partner management

A key real-world problem addressed by Collectify is managing a store with multiple partners.

Products and sales can be associated with their corresponding owners, making it easier to control:

- Individual inventory.
- Sales associated with products.
- Partner information.
- Earnings.
- Business performance.

### Sales management

The platform records sales and sale items, connecting transactions with products and owners so that the information can later be analyzed.

### AI business analysis

Collectify AI can analyze business information and answer questions such as:

> "¿Qué productos se están vendiendo mejor y cuáles debería considerar reabastecer?"

The objective is to turn operational data into useful business decisions.

### AI supplier discovery

A user can search for a product such as:

> "Funko Pokémon"

The AI-powered supplier workflow returns **structured supplier data**, such as:

- Supplier name.
- Description.
- Category.
- Location.
- Website.
- Rating or relevance when available.
- Verification information when available.
- Tags.

The Angular frontend consumes those structured results and renders supplier cards.

**The AI is therefore producing application data, not just conversational text.**

## AI architecture

Collectify separates two important AI workflows.

### Business analysis

```text
User
 ↓
Angular
 ↓
FastAPI
 ↓
Supabase
 ↓
Business data
 ↓
AI service
 ↓
Business recommendation
 ↓
Angular
```

### Supplier discovery

```text
User
 ↓
Supplier search
 ↓
FastAPI
 ↓
AI / supplier discovery
 ↓
Structured supplier results
 ↓
Angular
 ↓
Supplier cards
```

The important distinction is that supplier results are returned as structured data that the application can consume programmatically.

## Why this project matters

Small businesses generate valuable information every day, but that information is often distributed across spreadsheets, messages, notebooks, and manual processes.

Collectify aims to transform that operational information into a centralized system that can understand the business and help the people running it make better decisions.

**Inventory → Sales → Analysis → Recommendation → Action**

The long-term vision is not simply to digitize an inventory. It is to create an **AI-powered business intelligence layer for small retail businesses**.
